- Initial lens mirroring deep-research step 2b and experiment-designer step 0 market sizing.
- Five generic traps, all unverified by design: negative-unit-economics, top-down-tam,
  unsourced-willingness-to-pay, latency-over-correctness-floor, unbounded-free-tier. They are decided
  by arithmetic on the brief's figures with a current provider price page, or by research, not by a quote.
- Sources: census, BLS and Eurostat statistics and SEC EDGAR as examples of primary sizing data; a
  provider price page (freshness 30 days); response-time guidance (secondary).
- Required statements: customer-price-volume, correctness-floor, cost-to-serve.
- Sixteen cases: five planted/clean pairs and three omission pairs.
