# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-14
- Initial lens: 4 traps, all verified.
- Seed: LEG-05 (recorded as an alias on security-ethics.exploit-and-dump-to-prove); the seed bundles
  four behaviours, split here into exploiting and exposing real records to prove a vulnerability,
  scanning or probing unauthorised third-party systems, weaponising an exploit beyond confirming
  presence, and publishing vulnerability details before a fix.
- Verified against 18 U.S.C. 1030 (Cornell LII), the CISA Vulnerability Disclosure Policy Template and
  CVD process pages, the CERT/CC Guide to CVD (discovery and ethics pages), and GDPR Articles 4(12),
  32 and 33 (EUR-Lex).
- ISO/IEC 29147 (cited by the seed) is paywalled and its abstract page returns HTTP 403; it is listed
  as a source with a note but not quoted, so no trap depends on it. The CISA and CERT/CC texts carry
  the coordinated-disclosure rule instead.
- Report claims not carried as facts: the seed's "typically 90 days" embargo (no primary source; CISA
  states 45 days for unresponsive vendors) and GDPR Article 9 (only relevant for special-category data).
- Required statements: authorised targets, disclosure route, sensitive-data handling.
- Cases: a planted and clean twin per trap, plus one omission pair (sensitive-data-handling).
