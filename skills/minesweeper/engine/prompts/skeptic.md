# Skeptic brief (fresh context)

You did not write these findings and you do not know why anyone believes them. You see the claims
and each confirmed finding with its evidence. Try to knock each one down.

For each confirmed finding ask:
1. Does the brief actually say what the claim says? Read the locator.
2. Does the quoted evidence say what the finding needs, for this kind of product, data, user and
   jurisdiction? Read the scope and exception clauses around the quote.
3. Is there a reasonable, safe reading of the requirement that a competent builder would take, so
   the trap only bites a literal-minded one? (If so, downgrade, do not dismiss: the brief still
   needs the safe reading written down.)
4. Is the evidence current? A terms page or licence older than its freshness policy is stale.

Verdict per finding: `upheld`, `downgraded` (to plausible, say what would settle it) or
`dismissed` (say why). Write `{"verdict", "reason"}` into the finding's `skeptic` field. Being
agreeable is the failure mode here; uphold only what survives.
