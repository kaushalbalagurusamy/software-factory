# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-14
- Initial lens. Holds no company data: a per-brief research procedure (mission and values, customers
  and payer, regulators, incidents and enforcement, published policies, hiring signals) whose output
  goes to the project's docs/minesweep/company-<slug>.md.
- Four generic traps: contradicts-published-policy and ignores-sector-regulator are verified against
  15 U.S.C. 45 (govinfo.gov text); metric-serves-evaluator and assumed-data-rights are unverified
  (judgement trap; secondary FTC guidance and per-brief contracts decide).
- Required statements: organisation-and-payer, governing-regulators (regulatory-duties).
- Twelve cases: four planted/clean pairs and two omission pairs.
