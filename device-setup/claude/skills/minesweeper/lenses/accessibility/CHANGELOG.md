# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-14
- Initial lens: 4 traps, all verified.
- Traps: information or state conveyed by a single sense (colour-only); core functionality that is
  mouse-only or drag-only; a public-entity (ADA Title II) site or app below WCAG Level AA; an
  EAA-covered EU consumer service launched after 28 June 2025 without meeting Annex I.
- Verified against WCAG 2.2 (w3.org), the European Accessibility Act Directive (EU) 2019/882 (EUR-Lex
  via the EU Publications Office), and 28 CFR 35.200 (ADA Title II web rule).
- Note: 28 CFR 35.200 incorporates WCAG 2.1 Level AA by reference (not 2.2) and phases in by entity
  size; the 28 June 2025 EAA date is quoted from the Directive, not from the seed report.
- No report seed is assigned to accessibility; all four traps are generic.
- Required statements: accessibility standard/level, legal coverage, non-visual operability.
- Cases: a planted and clean twin per trap, plus one omission pair (a11y-standard).
