- Behaviour 7: counter reset after a passing stop, and whether the log line repeats on every post-cap call (C-08).
- Behaviour 4 for a frozen file that was deleted (C-09).
- R-15 "full-suite run" versus behaviour 5 "containing `checks.test`" for a single-file run (C-10).
