## Not tested and why
- Exact chain hash input and layout (C-19); global versus per-section chain (C-20). Tamper cases stay within one section and never recompute a hash.
- Removing the last entry of the ledger (C-21).
- How verify identifies the entry; only the section name is asserted (C-22).
- `now` type and time format; cases pass an aware UTC datetime and accept `Z` or `+00:00` (C-23).
- Audit Trail header role, a current ticket without a ledger file, whitespace in `current_ticket` (C-24); truncation before or after redaction (C-25).
